# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Practice: Un giorno in Italia" — Review episode
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Barista, Giulia, Venditore — reusing the native voices from episodes 1–3

**A1 skill focus:** none — this is a spaced-repetition practice episode. No new vocabulary.
It recombines the eight repertoire phrases from episodes 1–3 (bar, introductions, market) into
one continuous "day in Italy," so the listener has to *produce* everything from memory instead
of being taught it again.

**Pedagogy note:** per the curriculum's spaced-repetition plan, a practice episode goes in
every 2–3 new episodes to recombine prior content instead of introducing new content. This is
the first one, after episodes 1–3.

## Production notes
- **No new content** — every phrase here is a verbatim repeat of something taught in episodes
  1–3, in a new order. This is the point: the listener has to recall and produce it cold, not
  hear it re-taught.
- **Reused native voices**: Barista (Alessandro, ep 1), Giulia (Carlotta, ep 2), Venditore
  (Alessandro, ep 3) — same recurring cast across episodes.
- **Shorter gaps and no repeat-after-me**: since this episode only asks for recall, not fresh
  listening comprehension, the pauses are tighter and each drill is asked once instead of
  twice — a practice episode should move faster than a teaching one.
- **No scene ambience**: unlike episodes 1–3's persistent background scenes, this episode
  skips ambience entirely to keep focus on rapid-fire recall rather than atmosphere — a
  deliberate simplification for a review format.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, per episode
  1's fix, in both the rapid-review and full-day sections.
