# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Quanto costa?" — At the market
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Venditore (man), Cliente (woman) — native Italian speakers, at a fruit stall

**A1 skill focus:** asking for something and asking its price — the core of any shopping
interaction. Three repertoire phrases: "Vorrei...", "Quanto costa?", and "Ecco."

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as episodes 1–2 — no proactive pronunciation coaching,
full repertoire phrases only.

## Production notes
- **Venditore / Cliente voices**: Venditore reuses episode 1's Barista actor (Alessandro),
  Cliente reuses episode 1's Cliente actor (Carla) — same recurring native cast, new
  scenario, consistent with Marco/Giulia's reuse in episode 2.
- **Max's voice**: Vittorio throughout, verified up front (see episode 1's production notes
  for why this matters).
- **Scope**: three repertoire phrases (vorrei / quanto costa / ecco) built directly from the
  demo dialogue.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, per episode
  1's fix.
