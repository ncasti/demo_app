# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Quanti anni hai?" — Age and birthdays
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Luca (man), Sara (woman) — the pair from episodes 9 and 16, native
Italian speakers

**A1 skill focus:** asking and giving your age, and asking when someone's birthday is. Three
repertoire phrases: "Quanti anni hai?", "Ho trent'anni," and "Quando è il tuo compleanno?"

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as prior episodes — no proactive pronunciation
coaching, full repertoire phrases only.

## Production notes
- **Luca / Sara voices**: reprise their episode 9/16 actors (Stefano Ca, Carlotta) — listener
  continuity across episodes.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **Deliberate callback**: "Ho trent'anni" reuses the "ho" construction again (hotel,
  family, now age) — the fourth domain for the same grammar, by design.
