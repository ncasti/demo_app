# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Non capisco" — Asking for clarification
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Marco (man), Turista (woman) — native Italian speakers, on a street corner

**A1 skill focus:** the survival phrases every beginner needs the moment things go too fast.
Three repertoire phrases: "Non capisco," "Può ripetere?", and "Più lentamente, per favore."

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as prior episodes — no proactive pronunciation
coaching, full repertoire phrases only.

## Production notes
- **Marco / Turista voices**: Marco reprises his episode 2/6/19 actor (Stefano Ca); Turista
  reuses Carla — recurring native cast, new scenario.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **Why this episode exists now, not earlier**: these are meta-communication phrases that
  work regardless of topic, deliberately placed after twenty episodes' worth of vocabulary
  rather than at the very start — a learner needs something to not-understand yet before
  "I don't understand" is useful, and by episode 21 they have plenty.
