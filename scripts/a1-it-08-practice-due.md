# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Practice: Un pomeriggio in Italia" — Review episode
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Passante, Giulia, Cameriere — reusing the native voices from episodes 5–7

**A1 skill focus:** none — spaced-repetition practice episode. No new vocabulary. Recombines
the nine repertoire phrases from episodes 5–7 (directions, time, restaurant) into one
afternoon, so the listener produces everything from memory.

**Pedagogy note:** second practice episode in the curriculum's spaced-repetition plan
(episode 4 was the first, covering episodes 1–3).
