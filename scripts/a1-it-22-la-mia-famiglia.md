# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "La mia famiglia" — Talking about family
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Marco (man), Giulia (woman) — the pair from episodes 2, 6, 19, native
Italian speakers

**A1 skill focus:** introducing family members and asking about someone else's. Three
repertoire phrases: "Questa è mia sorella," "Ho un fratello," and "Hai fratelli?"

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as prior episodes — no proactive pronunciation
coaching, full repertoire phrases only.

## Production notes
- **Marco / Giulia voices**: reprise their episode 2/6/19 actors (Stefano Ca, Carlotta) —
  listener continuity across the arc.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **Deliberate callback**: "Ho un fratello" reuses the "ho" construction from episode 18's
  "Ho una prenotazione" — same grammar, new domain, spaced repetition of the underlying
  pattern rather than the phrase.
