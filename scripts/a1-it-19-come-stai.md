# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Come stai?" — Small talk and feelings
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Marco (man), Giulia (woman) — the pair from episodes 2 and 6, native
Italian speakers

**A1 skill focus:** asking how someone's doing and answering. Three repertoire phrases: "Come
stai?", "Sto bene," and "E tu?"

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as prior episodes — no proactive pronunciation
coaching, full repertoire phrases only. This is the last new-content episode of the arc —
episode 20 closes it out with a cumulative practice episode.

## Production notes
- **Marco / Giulia voices**: reprise their episode 2 and 6 actors (Stefano Ca, Carlotta) — a
  deliberate bookend for the last new-content episode of the arc.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **Round-trip variant**: like episodes 9 and 16, Giulia opens the exchange in-character —
  the natural trigger for a greeting question is hearing it asked.
