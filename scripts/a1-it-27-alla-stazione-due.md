# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Alla stazione, parte 2" — Finding your platform (chapter pilot, part 2 of 2)
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Impiegato (clerk, man) — native Italian speaker

**A1 skill focus:** opens by reviewing part 1's two phrases with a fresh city (Torino, never
drilled before), then adds one new phrase — no substitution pair needed for this one, a
chapter doesn't have to force every phrase into the same shape. New phrase: "Da che binario?"

**Pedagogy note:** second half of the pilot chapter. Review comes first, before any new
content, and uses a filler neither part 1 nor its own drills used — this is what makes it a
test of the underlying pattern rather than a repeat of a fixed sentence. Closes by chaining
all three phrases from both parts into one continuous transaction.

## Production notes
- **Impiegato's voice**: reuses Alessandro, same as part 1.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **No substitution pair for "Da che binario?"**: per the "don't force the structure" policy
  — this phrase doesn't have a natural filler slot to swap the way a city name or ticket type
  does, so it gets a single drill instead of a manufactured second variant.
- **Three fresh cities across the whole chapter, none repeated**: Roma and Milano in part 1's
  drills, Napoli in part 1's round-trip, Torino in part 2's review, Firenze in part 2's
  round-trip. Every single production of "un biglietto per..." used a different destination —
  the only way to actually confirm the pattern generalized instead of a sentence being
  memorized whole.
- **Registry updates**: `un_biglietto_per_roma` and `solo_andata` (both introduced in episode
  26) get a `review` recorded at `a1_it_27_alla_stazione_due`. `da_che_binario` is a new
  `topical` entry introduced here.
