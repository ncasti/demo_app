# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Practice: Il giro d'onore" — Final review episode
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4.5 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Receptionist, Barista, Giulia, Venditore — the recurring cast from the
whole arc

**A1 skill focus:** none — the sixth and final spaced-repetition practice episode of this
arc. Recombines episodes 18–19 (hotel, small talk) in the rapid review, then closes with a
"victory lap" round-trip that strings together callbacks from across the entire 20-episode
run: check in, get coffee, catch up with a friend, and shop at the market — one continuous
scene instead of an isolated drill.

**Pedagogy note:** the last practice episode gets a deliberately wider net than episodes 4,
8, 11, 14, and 17 — this is the arc's closing note, so it reaches back further than just the
two most recent episodes.

## Production notes
- **Wider recap than earlier practice episodes**: episodes 4, 8, 11, 14, and 17 each recap
  only their two or three immediately preceding episodes. This one deliberately reaches back
  to episode 1 for the closing scene, since it's the last episode of the run and should read
  as a capstone, not just another interval review.
- **Reused native voices**: Receptionist (Alessandro), Barista (Alessandro), Giulia
  (Carlotta), Venditore (Alessandro) — the full recurring cast, one last time.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **Closing line breaks the fourth wall slightly** ("that's the sample... real speech
  recognition, and a full curriculum") — appropriate only here, as the deliberate close of
  the scaling-test batch, not a pattern for regular episodes.
