# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Che ore sono?" — Telling time
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Marco (man), Giulia (woman) — the pair from episode 2, native Italian speakers

**A1 skill focus:** asking and telling the time, and excusing yourself when you have to go.
Three repertoire phrases: "Che ore sono?", "Sono le tre," and "Devo andare."

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as prior episodes — no proactive pronunciation
coaching, full repertoire phrases only.

## Production notes
- **Marco / Giulia voices**: same actors (Stefano Ca, Carlotta) reprising the episode 2
  characters, giving the listener continuity across episodes.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **Scope**: three repertoire phrases (che ore sono / sono le tre / devo andare) built
  directly from the demo dialogue.
