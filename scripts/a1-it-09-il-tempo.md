# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Che tempo fa?" — Talking about the weather
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Luca (man), Sara (woman) — native Italian speakers, friends outdoors

**A1 skill focus:** small talk about the weather. Three repertoire phrases: "Che tempo fa?",
"Fa caldo," and "C'è il sole."

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as prior episodes — no proactive pronunciation
coaching, full repertoire phrases only.

## Production notes
- **Luca / Sara voices**: reuse the Stefano Ca / Carlotta pairing under new character names —
  same recurring native cast, new scenario.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **Round-trip variant**: Sara asks her question in-character first, then Max narrates the
  instruction — a small structural variation from the usual instruction-first pattern, since
  here the natural conversational trigger is hearing her ask it.
