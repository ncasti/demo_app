# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Al telefono" — On the phone
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Elena — native Italian speaker, on the other end of the line

**A1 skill focus:** answering the phone and identifying yourself. Three repertoire phrases:
"Pronto?", "Sono...", and "Posso lasciare un messaggio?"

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as prior episodes — no proactive pronunciation
coaching, full repertoire phrases only.

## Production notes
- **Elena / Marco voices**: reuse the Carlotta and Stefano Ca actors — same recurring native
  cast, new scenario.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **No scene ambience** for the cold open — a phone ring/pickup effect stands in for a
  persistent background bed, since there's no physical location to hold ambience for.
- **Deliberate callback**: "Sono [name]" reuses the grammar from episode 2's "Io sono," in a
  new idiomatic context (phone identification drops "Io") — spaced repetition of the
  underlying structure, not just the phrase.
