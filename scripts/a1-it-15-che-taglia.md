# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Che taglia?" — Shopping for clothes
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Commessa (clerk, woman) — native Italian speaker, clothing shop

**A1 skill focus:** asking for an item and giving your size. Three repertoire phrases:
"Vorrei una maglietta," "Che taglia?", and "Taglia media."

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as prior episodes — no proactive pronunciation
coaching, full repertoire phrases only.

## Production notes
- **Commessa's voice**: reuses the Carla actor — same recurring native cast, new scenario.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **Deliberate callback**: "Vorrei" is the third domain this verb has appeared in (market,
  restaurant, now shopping) — spaced repetition of the same core construction across
  contexts, not just the same phrase repeated verbatim.
