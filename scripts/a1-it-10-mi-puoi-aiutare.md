# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Mi può aiutare?" — Asking for help
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Turista (woman), Passante (man) — native Italian speakers, on a street

**A1 skill focus:** asking a stranger for help and saying what you're looking for. Three
repertoire phrases: "Mi può aiutare?", "Cerco...", and "È qui vicino."

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as prior episodes — no proactive pronunciation
coaching, full repertoire phrases only.

- **Scope**: three repertoire phrases (mi può aiutare / cerco / è qui vicino) built directly
  from the demo dialogue.
