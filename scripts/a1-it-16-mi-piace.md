# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Mi piace" — Saying what you like
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Luca (man), Sara (woman) — the pair from episode 9, native Italian speakers

**A1 skill focus:** saying what you like, don't like, and asking someone else. Three
repertoire phrases: "Mi piace...", "Non mi piace...", and "Ti piace...?"

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as prior episodes — no proactive pronunciation
coaching, full repertoire phrases only.

## Production notes
- **Luca / Sara voices**: reprise their episode 9 actors (Stefano Ca, Carlotta) — listener
  continuity across episodes.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **Round-trip variant**: like episode 9, Luca asks his question in-character first, since
  that's the natural conversational trigger — the listener answers rather than initiating.
