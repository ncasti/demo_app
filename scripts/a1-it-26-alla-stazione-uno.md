# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Alla stazione, parte 1" — Buying a ticket (chapter pilot, part 1 of 2)
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Impiegato (clerk, man), Cliente (woman) — native Italian speakers

**A1 skill focus:** buying a ticket to a destination and specifying one-way or round-trip.
Two repertoire phrases this time, not three — each drilled with a substitution variant, not
just repeated verbatim: "Un biglietto per Roma, per favore" (+ substitution: "...per Milano"),
and "Solo andata" (+ substitution: "Andata e ritorno").

**Pedagogy note — this is the new chapter template, piloted:** episodes 1–25 taught 2–3
phrases once and relied on periodic practice episodes for review. Feedback: too much new
content too fast, not enough repetition per phrase. This episode is part 1 of a 2-part
chapter — part 2 reviews these phrases (with fresh substitutions, not the same ones) before
teaching anything new, and ends by chaining both parts' phrases into one transaction. See
`audio-gen/phrase_registry.json` for how these phrases get tracked for future spaced review,
and `audio-gen/README.md`'s "Curriculum structure" section for the full policy.

## Production notes
- **Impiegato / Cliente voices**: reuse Alessandro and Carla — same recurring native cast as
  episode 13's version of this scenario.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **New in this episode — substitution drills**: each phrase gets a second challenge with a
  different filler (Roma → Milano, solo andata → andata e ritorno) instead of repeating the
  identical sentence a second time. The reverse-translate and round-trip sections then use a
  *third* city (Napoli) never drilled directly, to test whether the pattern generalized
  rather than whether a specific sentence got memorized.
- **Registry**: both phrases registered in `phrase_registry.json` as `topical`, introduced in
  this episode. Part 2 will record a `review` for each when it opens with recall of them.
