# A1 Audio Lesson — Sample Script (Italian)

**Title:** "Prendo un taxi" — Catching a taxi
**Level:** A1 (total beginner)
**Runtime target:** ~4 minutes
**Hosts:** Clara (IT/EN bilingual) & Max (IT/EN bilingual)
**Scenario voices:** Tassista (man) — native Italian speaker, taxi driver

**A1 skill focus:** giving a destination, asking how long it takes, and asking the driver to
wait. Three repertoire phrases: "Mi porta a...?", "Quanto ci vuole?", and "Può aspettare?"

**Pedagogy note:** same standing policy as prior episodes — no proactive pronunciation
coaching, full repertoire phrases only.

## Production notes
- **Tassista's voice**: reuses the Alessandro actor — same recurring native cast, new
  scenario.
- **Cue order**: prompt → SPEAKER SFX → pause (attempt) → CORRECT SFX → answer, throughout.
- **Scene accent sounds**: a car door closing/engine start at the top, indicator clicking off
  at the end — a taxi's natural equivalent of the bar's door chime or the hotel's elevator
  ping.
